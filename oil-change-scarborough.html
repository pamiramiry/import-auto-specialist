Oil Changes, Done Properly

A regular oil change is the cheapest insurance you can buy for your engine. We change your oil and filter using the correct grade for your vehicle (full-synthetic or conventional, whatever your car actually calls for) and reset the service reminder. While it's up on the hoist, we do a quick visual check of belts, hoses, fluids, tires, and brakes, and give you an honest heads-up on anything worth watching. No pressure, no upselling.

The right oil spec matters more than it used to, especially on import engines. Many European makes require a specific synthetic grade and manufacturer approval. We use oil that meets your maker's spec, so your engine stays properly protected.

Short trips and stop-and-go GTA traffic count as "severe service," which can call for more frequent changes than the easy-highway interval suggests. If most of your driving is around Scarborough, it's worth keeping an eye on.
