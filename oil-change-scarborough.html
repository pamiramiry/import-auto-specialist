Signs Your Oil Change Is Overdue

Most cars will tell you when they are ready for a service, either through a reminder on the dash or through how the engine behaves. Any of these is worth acting on:

  • An oil change or service reminder lit on the dash
  • Oil on the dipstick that looks dark, gritty, or sludgy
  • The oil level reading low between services
  • A noisier engine, especially a ticking sound on startup
  • An oil smell in the cabin or spots on the driveway
  • You have lost track of when the last change was done

If the oil pressure warning light comes on while you are driving, that is different and more urgent. Stop when it is safe to do so and call (416) 913-2394 rather than continuing to drive.

Oil Changes, Done Properly

A regular oil change is the cheapest insurance you can buy for your engine. We change your oil and filter using the correct grade for your vehicle (full-synthetic or conventional, whatever your car actually calls for) and reset the service reminder. While it's up on the hoist, we do a quick visual check of belts, hoses, fluids, tires, and brakes, and give you an honest heads-up on anything worth watching. No pressure, no upselling.

The drain plug and filter housing get fresh gaskets or crush washers where the vehicle calls for them, which is the small detail that prevents a slow seep showing up on your driveway a month later. We fill to the correct level, run the engine, and check underneath before the car comes off the hoist.

Why Putting It Off Gets Expensive

Engine oil does more than lubricate. It carries heat away from the bearings, holds combustion by-products in suspension, and keeps the inside of the engine clean. As it ages it loses viscosity and the additive package wears out, so all three jobs get done worse at once.

Left long enough, tired oil turns to sludge and starts blocking the narrow oil galleries that feed the top of the engine. On modern engines that also means starving the variable valve timing and turbocharger of clean oil, and those are the parts that turn a routine service into a four-figure repair. An oil change is one of the least expensive things you will ever do to a car; a seized turbo or a spun bearing is one of the most expensive.

Scarborough Driving Counts as Severe Service

Most owner's manuals list two maintenance schedules: normal and severe. A lot of GTA drivers are on the severe schedule without realising it. Short trips where the engine never fully warms up, stop-and-go traffic along Warden and Eglinton, idling in winter, and the temperature swing between a −20 °C January and a humid August all count.

Short trips are the hardest of the lot, because moisture and fuel that get into the oil never burn off if the engine does not reach full operating temperature. If most of your driving is local errands rather than highway runs, it is worth changing your oil closer to the shorter end of the recommended interval.

The Right Oil Spec for Import Engines

Oil specification matters more than it used to, and it matters most on import engines. Many European makes require a specific manufacturer approval rather than just a viscosity grade, and using an oil that merely looks similar on the bottle can affect emissions equipment and, on some engines, timing chain wear.

As an import-focused shop we use oil that meets your maker's actual specification rather than a generic bulk fill, and we fit a filter appropriate to the engine. If you are not sure what your car takes, call (416) 913-2394 with your year, make, and model and we will tell you.

Oil Change FAQ

Common Oil Change Questions

How often should I change my oil?

It depends on your vehicle and oil type. Always follow the interval in your owner's manual. Short trips and city driving are harder on oil and can call for more frequent changes.

Do you use synthetic oil?

Yes. We match your manufacturer's specification, whether full-synthetic or conventional, so your engine gets exactly the oil it's designed for.

How long does an oil change take?

A straightforward oil and filter change is usually done well within an hour, including the multi-point visual check. Call (416) 913-2394 ahead of your visit and we'll let you know the best time to come in so you're not waiting.

Do I need to change the oil filter every time?

Yes, and we always do. A used filter is already holding the debris it captured from the old oil, so leaving it in place puts that straight back into your fresh oil. The filter is included in every oil change we do.
