Porsche Diagnostics With PIWIS

We run PIWIS, the factory tester Porsche dealers use. Very few independent shops have it, and on a modern Porsche it is close to essential.

These cars are as module-dependent as anything else built in Germany, and more sensitive than most about what has been configured correctly. PIWIS reads Porsche-specific fault codes across every control unit, runs the manufacturer's guided diagnostics, and performs the adaptations and resets a repair requires afterwards. A generic scanner reads the powertrain and leaves the rest of the car unread.

It also means we can tell you what is actually wrong before quoting. On a Porsche the gap between a sensor and the component it monitors can be several thousand dollars, and that is not a distinction worth guessing at.

Common Porsche Repairs

  • Check engine lights and Porsche-specific fault codes
  • Oil leaks, cooling system faults and water pumps
  • Coil packs, misfires and running complaints
  • Suspension, including air suspension on Cayenne and Panamera
  • Brakes, wear sensors and brake fluid service
  • Electrical and module faults, battery drain and no-starts
  • Air conditioning and heating
  • Body and collision repair with colour-matched paint

We diagnose first and quote second on all of it. If your car is showing a specific message, read it out when you call and we will tell you honestly whether it is something to drive on or not.

Servicing, Fluids and Intervals

Porsche specifies particular oil approvals, and on these engines the specification is not a suggestion. We service to the approval your engine requires, reset the interval properly, and handle brake fluid, coolant and transmission fluid on the schedule the car actually calls for rather than a generic one.

Brakes deserve their own mention. Porsche brake systems are expensive and they are also the system most affected by how the car is driven, so we measure and show you rather than recommending on age alone. Routine work happens here too: brake repair, tires and wheel alignment, and oil changes.

Alignment, Tires and How These Cars Sit

Porsches run tighter alignment tolerances than most road cars and frequently use staggered tire setups with different sizes front and rear. Fitting something merely similar, or aligning to a generic specification, changes how the car behaves in a way an owner will feel immediately.

Low-profile tires also transmit far more of a pothole strike into the wheel and suspension, which matters on Scarborough roads in spring. If you have hit something hard, it is worth having the alignment and the wheels checked rather than waiting for uneven wear to show up.

Independent Rates, Factory-Level Tools

Out of factory coverage, the maintenance cost of a Porsche is what most owners weigh, and the fear about going independent is that the shop cannot read the car properly and will replace parts by elimination. PIWIS is the answer to that. You also get body and collision repair in the same building, and an estimate before any work begins.

Porsche FAQ

Common Porsche Questions

Do you have Porsche factory diagnostic equipment?

Yes. We run PIWIS, the factory tester Porsche dealers use. It reads Porsche-specific fault codes across every control unit rather than the powertrain alone, runs the manufacturer’s guided diagnostics, and performs the adaptations and resets a repair requires afterwards.

Which Porsche models do you work on?

Porsche models across the range, both current and older. Call (416) 913-2394 with your model and year and we will confirm what we can read and service on your specific car before you book.

Can you align a Porsche to factory specification?

Yes. These cars run tighter tolerances than most road cars and many use staggered tire setups with different sizes front and rear, so a generic alignment is not good enough. We align to the specification your model requires and will tell you if a wheel or suspension component is bent rather than simply adjusting around it.

Are you a Porsche dealer or authorised centre?

No. We are an independent shop on Warden Ave. running since 2004, with a licensed 310S technician and PIWIS diagnostic equipment. We are not affiliated with or authorised by Porsche.
