FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers based on confirmed shop details. For anything not listed, please call (416) 913-2394.

Where is Import Auto Specialist located?

We're located at 777 Warden Ave., Scarborough, ON M1L 4C2, near Warden Avenue in Scarborough.

What are your business hours?

We're open Monday to Friday from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., and closed on Saturday and Sunday.

How do I find out what my vehicle needs?

Call (416) 913-2394 or fill out the inspection request form on this page with your vehicle details and a description of the issue. The team will look the vehicle over and explain what it needs, with a clear estimate before any work begins.

Do you service all makes and models?

Yes. We repair and maintain import and domestic vehicles alike, with particular expertise in imports. Call the shop to confirm service for your specific make and model.

How can I contact the shop?

Call (416) 913-2394 during business hours, or use the inspection request form on this page and the team will follow up.

Do you use dealer diagnostic tools?

Yes. We use dealer-level diagnostic tools, the same kind of software the dealerships use, so we can read your car's systems the way the dealer does. You get that at independent-shop rates.

Do you use OEM parts?

Usually, yes. Most jobs get OEM parts, but it depends on the repair and the part. If you want to know exactly what's going on your car, ask us and we'll tell you before the work starts.

Is the shop open on weekends?

No, we're open Monday to Friday and closed on Saturday and Sunday. Please call during weekday hours to arrange service.
