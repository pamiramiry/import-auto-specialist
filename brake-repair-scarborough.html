Signs You Need Your Brakes Checked

Brakes rarely fail all at once. They warn you first. If you notice any of these, it's worth booking an inspection soon:

  • Squealing or squeaking when you brake
  • Grinding or a metal-on-metal sound
  • A soft, spongy, or sinking brake pedal
  • The car pulling to one side when stopping
  • Vibration or shuddering through the pedal
  • Noticeably longer stopping distances
  • A brake warning light on the dash
  • A burning smell after braking hard

Not sure how urgent it is? Call (416) 913-2394 and describe what you're hearing or feeling, and we'll tell you whether it's safe to keep driving until your appointment.

What a Brake Service Involves

Every visit starts with a proper inspection rather than a guess. We measure your brake pad thickness, check the rotors for scoring, warping, and minimum thickness, and inspect the calipers, hoses, brake lines, and fluid. A short road test helps us confirm the symptom you described.

Once we know what's actually going on, we walk you through the findings and give you a clear quote before any work starts. Depending on what the inspection shows, that can mean new pads and rotors, caliper service, replacing worn hardware, or a brake fluid flush, but only what your vehicle genuinely needs.

Why It Pays to Fix Brakes Early

Brakes are the one system on your car you never want to gamble with. Worn pads mean longer stopping distances and less control, especially in the wet or on ice. There's a cost angle too: pads left too long wear down to the metal and start chewing into the rotors, turning an affordable pad replacement into a bigger rotor job. Catching it early almost always costs less.

Brakes and Scarborough Roads

Local driving is genuinely hard on brakes. Winter road salt speeds up corrosion on rotors, calipers, and brake lines. Stop-and-go traffic along Warden, Eglinton, and the 401 wears pads faster than steady highway driving, and rough, pothole-scarred winter roads add extra stress. If you drive in the GTA year-round, regular brake checks are worth the habit.
