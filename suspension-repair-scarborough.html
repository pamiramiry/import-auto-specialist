Signs Your Suspension Needs Attention

Suspension wear tends to creep up gradually, so it's easy to get used to. Any of these are worth getting checked:

  • A bouncy, floaty, or unusually harsh ride
  • Clunking or knocking over bumps
  • The car pulling to one side
  • Uneven or cupped tire wear
  • The nose dipping sharply when you brake
  • Steering that feels loose or wanders
  • Vibration through the wheel or seat
  • The car leaning heavily through corners

Noticing a clunk or a pull after a rough road? Call (416) 913-2394 and we'll get it inspected before a worn part takes others with it.

What Suspension & Steering Service Involves

We inspect the whole system (shocks and struts, springs, control arms, bushings, ball joints, tie rods, and sway-bar links), checking each for play, leaks, and corrosion. A road test confirms the noise or handling issue you described, and we check the alignment, since worn parts and poor alignment usually go hand in hand.

Once the inspection is done, we show you what's worn and give you a clear estimate before any work begins. You get a straight answer on what needs doing now versus what can safely wait, never a blanket "replace everything."

Why It's Worth Fixing Promptly

Suspension is about far more than comfort. It keeps your tires planted for steering, braking, and control, and that matters most on wet or icy roads. Worn components also wear tires unevenly, so putting it off often means buying new tires sooner than you should have to. Handling it promptly protects both your safety and your wallet.

Scarborough Roads Are Hard on Suspension

If your suspension feels tired, local roads are a big part of the story. Potholes and frost-heaved winter pavement across Scarborough and the wider GTA pound shocks, struts, and bushings, while road salt slowly corrodes suspension and steering parts. Rough roads are exactly why these components tend to wear out here faster than the maintenance interval suggests.

Suspension FAQ

Common Suspension Questions

How do I know if I need shocks or struts?

Shocks and struts do a similar job, and different vehicles use one or the other. The warning signs are alike: a bouncy ride, poor handling, and uneven tire wear. We inspect your vehicle to tell you which it uses and exactly what's worn.

How long does suspension repair take?

It depends on which components are worn and parts availability for your vehicle. Some jobs are same-day, others take longer. Call (416) 913-2394 and we'll give you a realistic timeframe once we've inspected it.

Do you service air suspension on European imports?

Yes. We diagnose and repair air suspension on many European models. Call with your make and model and we'll confirm service for your vehicle.

Can potholes really damage my suspension?

Yes. A hard pothole hit can bend control arms, damage struts, crack bushings, or knock your alignment out, sometimes all at once. If your car started clunking or pulling after a rough road, it's worth an inspection.
