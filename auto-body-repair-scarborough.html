Why Fix Body Damage and Rust Early

Small damage rarely stays small. A chip or scratch that breaks the paint lets moisture in, and in a salted winter climate that turns into rust that spreads under the panel. A cracked or misaligned bumper stops protecting the car the way it should. Repairing damage early keeps it from growing into a bigger, costlier job — and protects your vehicle's value.

Body Damage, Rust, and Scarborough Roads

Local driving is hard on a car's body. Winter road salt accelerates rust on rockers, wheel arches, and seams; tight lots and street parking mean door dings and bumper scuffs; and potholes and road debris chip paint. If you drive in the GTA year-round, catching body damage and rust early is worth the habit.

Colour-Matched Paint for Import & Domestic Vehicles

Import and domestic finishes alike need the right colour match to look factory-correct after a repair. We refinish to your vehicle's paint code so repaired areas blend in — on European, Japanese, and domestic vehicles alike.
