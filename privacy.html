Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains what information the Import Auto Specialist website collects, why, who receives it, and the choices you have. Import Auto Specialist is an auto repair shop at 777 Warden Ave., Scarborough, ON M1L 4C2. The website is informational and has one form: the inspection request form.

What we collect

If you send an inspection request, we receive what you type into the form:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your vehicle (year, make and model)
  • Your description of the issue or the service you need

When you visit the site, some technical information is also collected automatically by the services described below: for example your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you view, the site that referred you, and an approximate location based on your IP address.

If you tap our phone number, the call is made by your own phone. The website does not receive your phone number; analytics only records that a phone link was tapped.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request and arrange service for your vehicle.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how people find and use the site so we can improve it.
  • To deliver the website and keep it working and secure.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or add you to any mailing list.

Who receives it

We use a small number of service providers to run the website:

  • Web3Forms receives inspection requests and delivers them to our business email inbox. See the Web3Forms privacy policy.
  • Google Analytics (Google) measures how the site is used. See Analytics below.
  • Google Maps (Google) shows the map in the Contact section of our home page. When the map loads, Google receives technical information such as your IP address and browser details, and may set its own cookies, under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel hosts the website. Like any web host, it processes technical information such as IP addresses in its server logs to deliver and protect the site.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

Processing outside Canada

These providers may store or process information in the United States and other countries. Information held outside Canada is subject to the laws of the country where it is held, which may allow authorities there to access it.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to measure traffic to the site. It uses cookies and an online identifier to collect information such as the pages you view, how long you stay, how far you scroll, the site that referred you, your approximate region, and your device and browser. We also count a few actions: tapping a phone number, email link or directions link, using the main buttons and menus, and successfully sending the inspection request form (that it was sent, not what you wrote).

We do not send your name, phone number, email address or form contents to Google Analytics. Google Signals and ad personalization are turned off, we do not use Google Analytics for advertising or remarketing, and we do not use User-ID.

Turning analytics off: use the button below or the "Turn off analytics" link at the bottom of any page. Google Analytics then stops loading on this site in this browser, and its cookies are cleared where your browser allows. Your choice is saved in this browser, so you will need to set it again if you clear your browsing data or use another browser or device. You can turn analytics back on the same way. You can also block cookies in your browser or use Google's opt-out browser add-on. The site works normally either way.

Cookies and browser storage

This is what the site itself sets in your browser:

  • _ga (cookie, Google Analytics): distinguishes visitors. Google lists its lifetime as 2 years; some browsers, including Chrome, cap it at 400 days. Not set when analytics is turned off.
  • _ga_LSB3NXXYZD (cookie, Google Analytics): keeps track of your session. Same lifetime as _ga. Not set when analytics is turned off.
  • ias-analytics-off (browser storage): remembers that you turned analytics off. Stays until you turn analytics back on or clear your browsing data.
  • ias-analytics-notice-ok (browser storage): remembers that you closed the analytics notice. Stays until you clear your browsing data.

The embedded Google Map on the home page may set Google's own cookies when it loads. We do not set any other cookies ourselves.

How long we keep information

We keep inspection requests only as long as we need them to respond to you and follow up on your request, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it or stop using it, subject to any legal requirements to keep certain records. To make a request or ask a question about privacy, call us at (416) 913-2394 or write to us at Import Auto Specialist, 777 Warden Ave., Scarborough, ON M1L 4C2.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or the services we use change. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
