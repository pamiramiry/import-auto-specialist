Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Import Auto Specialist ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains, in plain language, what information this website collects and how we use it. We keep this simple because our website is simple: it is an informational site with a single estimate request form.

What we collect

The only information we collect from you is what you choose to enter in the estimate request form on our website. That may include:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your vehicle (year, make & model)
  • The description of the issue or service you enter

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone else.

How we use your information

We use the details you submit for one purpose only: to respond to your inquiry and arrange service for your vehicle. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information, and we do not share it with third parties except as needed to operate the form (for example, the email/form service that delivers your request to us) or where required by law.

Data retention

We keep estimate requests only as long as needed to respond to you and to maintain reasonable records of the service relationship. You may ask us to delete your information at any time by contacting us.

Cookies & analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors find and use the site, so we can improve it. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser and collects information such as the pages you view, how long you spend on them, the approximate region you are browsing from, and the type of device and browser you use. We also record when a visitor taps one of the phone number links, so we can tell which parts of the site actually help people reach us.

This information is used only to measure and improve the website. It is not used for advertising, we do not sell it, and it is not linked to the details you submit through the estimate request form. Google processes this data under Google's own privacy policy.

You can opt out at any time by installing Google's browser opt-out add-on, or by blocking cookies in your browser settings. The site works normally either way.

Pages that display a map use an embedded Google Map, which is also subject to Google's own privacy terms.
